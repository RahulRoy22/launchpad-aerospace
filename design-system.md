## Brand & Visual Tone
The visual aesthetic fuses dark-mode precision engineering with luminous deep-space optics. It communicates absolute structural reliability, cutting-edge propulsion technology, and computational rigor:
- Deep space atmospheric canvas with localized radiant violet-to-cyan telemetry glows.
- Translucent dark-indigo paneling overlaid with hairline borders simulating composite avionics casing.
- Crisp typographic clarity where clean geometric sans-serif meets technical monospaced mission data.
- High visual tension between deep black/midnight voids and concentrated energetic purple plasma illumination.
